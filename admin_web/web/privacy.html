Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026  ·  Effective immediately upon access

1. Overview & Scope

ExpenseSplit Pro is a closed, internal expense management application developed for group expense splitting, OCR-based receipt parsing, and financial audit trail management. This Privacy Policy applies to all users of both the mobile application and the administrative web portal.

Access to this platform is restricted exclusively to pre-authorised personnel. No public user registration is offered.

2. Data We Collect

We collect only the minimum data required to operate the platform:

  • Authentication data: Google account email address and display name, used solely for login via Google OAuth 2.0.
  • Expense records: Transaction amounts, categories, dates, descriptions, and group split allocations entered by users.
  • Receipt images: Photos uploaded or captured for OCR parsing to extract expense details automatically.
  • Audit logs: Administrative action records (e.g., settings changes, user modifications) timestamped and stored for compliance.
  • Phone number (optional): If a user links a phone number for 2FA recovery purposes.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To authenticate users and manage access control within the platform.
  • To record, split, and track shared group expenses between authorised members.
  • To parse receipt images via OCR and automatically populate expense fields.
  • To generate internal financial reports and analytics dashboards.
  • To maintain an immutable audit trail for system compliance and accountability.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond the internal scope described above.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

Your data is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties for commercial purposes. The following third-party services are used solely to operate the platform:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Storage): Secure backend infrastructure for authentication, database, and file storage.
  • Google OAuth 2.0: Used exclusively for sign-in; we receive only your email address and display name.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Data retention periods are configurable by administrators in the Privacy & Data Governance settings panel. Upon account deletion or administrator request, all personally identifiable information is permanently removed from our systems within 30 days, except where legal obligations require longer retention.

6. Security

  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS encryption.
  • Firebase Security Rules enforce role-based access control at the database level.
  • Optional PII masking (configurable by admins) hides sensitive email addresses in administrative lists.
  • Administrative actions are logged immutably to prevent unauthorised modifications.

7. Your Rights (PDPA / GDPR)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Object to or restrict certain types of processing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact your system administrator directly.

8. Contact

For privacy-related enquiries, data access requests, or to report a concern, please contact your designated system administrator. This platform is operated internally and does not have a public-facing support channel.
